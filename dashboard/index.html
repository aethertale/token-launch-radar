Method note

Snapshot as of: loading.

A protocol is on this board when its snapshot record has no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol — it records no token. The watchlist is the floor: every protocol at or above $50,000,000 TVL that records no token is on it, and the radar adds the 160 strongest pre-token protocols below the floor, ranked by TVL. Protocols whose token already trades are listed separately at their market cap and are never ranked. Age is the time since the protocol was first listed; when the snapshot gives no listing date, the model counts 365 days. Implied value is an estimate of what a token could be worth if it launched at its peers' valuation — not a price, not a promise, and not investment advice.

In plain words, for the judge: take the protocol's locked value, multiply it by what similar protocols' tokens are actually worth relative to their locked value, nudge it up or down a little for last week's growth, and nudge it up a little more the longer the protocol has run without a token. That is the whole model, and every number it uses is printed on this page.
